Sunday Buttermilk Pancakes

Ingredients: 2 cups flour, 2 cups buttermilk, 2 eggs, 2 tablespoons
sugar, 1 teaspoon baking soda and 3 tablespoons melted butter.

Method: Whisk the wet and dry ingredients separately, then combine them
with a few strokes; small lumps are fine. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
Cook 1/4 cup of batter per pancake on a medium-hot pan until bubbles
form, then flip.

Serving: Serve with maple syrup and sliced strawberries. Leftover batter
keeps for one day in the fridge.
