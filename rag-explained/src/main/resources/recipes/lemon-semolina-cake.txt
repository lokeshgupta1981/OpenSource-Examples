Nani's Lemon Semolina Cake

Ingredients: 1 cup fine semolina, 3/4 cup sugar, 1 cup yogurt, 1/2 cup
melted butter, zest of 2 lemons, 1 teaspoon baking powder and a pinch of
cardamom.

Method: Mix the semolina, sugar and yogurt, and let the batter rest for
20 minutes so the semolina softens. Stir in the butter, zest, cardamom
and baking powder. Bake at 170 C for 38 minutes in a 9-inch tin.

Serving: Pour warm lemon syrup over the cake while it is still hot. The
cake keeps for 3 days in an airtight box at room temperature.
