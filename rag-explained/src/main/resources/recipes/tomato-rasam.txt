Amma's Tomato Rasam

Ingredients: 4 ripe tomatoes, 2 tablespoons tamarind pulp, 1 teaspoon
rasam powder, 4 cloves of garlic, curry leaves, mustard seeds and fresh
coriander.

Method: Boil the chopped tomatoes with tamarind pulp, rasam powder and 3
cups of water for 10 minutes. Fry mustard seeds, crushed garlic and
curry leaves in ghee and pour them into the pot. Turn off the heat as
soon as it foams.

Serving: Serve the rasam hot with steamed rice, or drink it from a cup
when you have a cold.
