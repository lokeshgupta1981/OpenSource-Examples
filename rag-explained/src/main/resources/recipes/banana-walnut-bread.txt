Uncle Ravi's Banana Walnut Bread

Ingredients: 3 very ripe bananas, 2 cups flour, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 2
eggs, 1/3 cup oil, 1 teaspoon baking soda and 1 cup chopped walnuts.

Method: Mash the bananas, then whisk in the eggs, sugar and oil. Fold in
the flour, baking soda and walnuts without overmixing. Bake at 180 C for
55 minutes in a loaf tin.

Serving: Let the bread cool for an hour before slicing. Toast leftover
slices and spread them with butter.
